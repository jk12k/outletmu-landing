# Outletmu Landing Page — Image Generation Prompts

Dokumen ini berisi prompt siap copy untuk generate foto landing page Outletmu.
Setiap prompt menyertakan tag referensi visual dari folder `referensi1/`.

## Brand & Color Tokens
- Background light: cream/white
- Accent primary: teal/green `#22C5A2` to `#1AB494`
- Header/Text: navy `#1B2A4E`
- Alert: oranye lembut, merah halus untuk negative
- Logo refs:
  - `referensi1/logo-wordmark-light.png` — header light
  - `referensi1/logo-icon-light.png` — favicon, watermark light
  - `referensi1/logo-full-dark.png` — footer dark, OG dark

## Negative Prompt (Global)
ugly hands, distorted fingers, plastic skin, fake plastic food, harsh studio flash, watermark, fake competitor logos, gibberish text on screen, fake QR noise, generic stock photo look, western corporate office, european setting, cartoon, illustration, 3d render look, sterile lab feel, AI artifact look, low quality, neon, glitch, oversaturated colors
